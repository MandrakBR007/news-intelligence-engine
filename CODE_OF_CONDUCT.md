# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free environment for everyone,
regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Focus on the problem, not the person.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy and do not share others' data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive or bad-faith behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban participants who violate this code. Reports can be sent to the
maintainers through a private channel; reports will be handled confidentially.
